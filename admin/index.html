Free Payment Gateway Guide (India)

To replace our simulated payment flow with a live, real checkout system, you can use these zero-cost developer setups in India:

💳

Option 1: Razorpay Standard (Recommended for UPI/Cards)

  1. Sign up for a free developer account at razorpay.com.
  2. Verify your email to enter Test Mode instantly. No company registration or KYC is required to test!
  3. Go to Settings > API Keys and click "Generate Test Key". Copy the key_id.
  4. Include Razorpay SDK script: <script src="https://checkout.razorpay.com/v1/checkout.js"></script>.
  5. Initialize with your test key to open a beautiful dynamic UPI, Card, and Netbanking popup for customers.
✨

Option 2: Free UPI QR Direct Integration (Free Forever, No Code APIs)

For zero-transaction-fee Indian UPI payments, you can generate standard UPI intent links directly on your dashboard without any middleman provider:

upi://pay?pa=yourvpa@upi&pn=OwnerName&am=DynamicAmount&cu=INR

In your final code, generate a QR code pointing to this URL. Customers scan using GPAY, PhonePe, or Paytm. When they pay, funds hit your bank account instantly with 0% fee.

🌍

Option 3: Stripe India developer keys

  1. Create an account at stripe.com/in.
  2. Test mode is active by default. Copy your Publishable API key.
  3. Stripe works for card checkout. Razorpay remains superior for Indian UPI flows.
